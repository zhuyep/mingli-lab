# Community conduct

Discuss rules and evidence respectfully. Different schools and interpretations may coexist when clearly labeled. Do not harass people, disclose private birth data, make discriminatory judgments, or promote harmful forecasts. Critique claims and implementations rather than people.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that expose personal data, infringe licenses, spam the project or violate these expectations. For a private concern, request a private contact route without publishing sensitive details.
