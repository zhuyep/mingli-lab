## 这一卷，有什么新变化？ · What's inside this edition?

- **墨色与留白 / Ink and space**<br>
  黑白手绘风插画、自托管中文手写标题与清晰正文，像翻开一本安静的旧书。插画为项目原创 AI 辅助作品，并非古籍扫描。<br>
  Original AI-assisted ink artwork, self-hosted Chinese handwriting for headings and legible body text evoke a quiet old book. The illustration is not an antique scan.
- **六章白话 / Six readable chapters**<br>
  从“你的底色”到“给这一年的提醒”，每章都有生活例子和一个留给自己的问题。<br>
  From your underlying pattern to questions for the year, each chapter offers an everyday example and a reflection prompt.
- **每一步，都能追问 / Reasoning within reach**<br>
  展开月令、根气、透干、条件化平衡路径、关系线索、流年与可选大运；未知的信息保持未知。<br>
  Inspect month context, roots, visible stems, conditional balance paths, relationships, annual pillars and optional traditional cycles. Unknown information stays unknown.
- **先试，再决定 / Try before you commit**<br>
  两项主要输入，也可不填个人信息直接看示例。中英界面、三项本地问答、十神推演与 23 点换日对照均保留。<br>
  Two primary inputs, or a no-input sample. Includes Chinese/English UI, three guided local questions, ten-god reasoning and a midnight/23:00 comparison.
- **把命书留在自己手里 / Keep your own copy**<br>
  导出 Markdown 命书、SVG 卡片或 JSON；不调用云模型、不上传生辰、不在浏览器存储命盘。导出仍可能含个人信息，请自行保管。<br>
  Export Markdown, SVG or JSON. No cloud model calls, birth-data uploads or browser chart storage. Exports may still contain personal information; keep them private.

## 口径与留白 · Conventions and open questions

v0.4.0 是公开预览版，用于传统文化体验与自我反思，不作命运、健康、投资或婚姻预测。强弱筛选为已公开的项目启发式；未知时辰、杂气月和部分集中结构不强行下结论。公历输入限 1901–2099、固定 UTC+8，不自动转换出生地、夏令时、真太阳时或农历。

v0.4.0 is a public preview for cultural exploration and reflection, not predictions about fate, health, investments or marriage. Strength screening is a documented project heuristic; unknown hours, mixed Earth months and some concentrated charts withhold judgment. Gregorian input supports 1901–2099 at fixed UTC+8, without automatic birthplace, daylight-saving, apparent-solar-time or lunar-date conversion.

发布时的验收记录：24 项规则/导出测试、类型/构建/格式检查，以及 25 项浏览器检查，覆盖窄屏布局、日期切换、依据展开、本地字体、下载与未知时辰。软件检查不证明预测有效，也不等于独立用户采用。

Checks recorded for this release: 24 rule/export tests, type/build/format checks and 25 browser checks covering narrow layouts, date transitions, expandable reasoning, local fonts, downloads and unknown-hour behavior. Software checks establish neither predictive validity nor independent adoption.
